Privacy Policy

Stickman Showdown, by Quillstorm Studios · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Stickman Showdown ("the Game"), published by Quillstorm Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: the Game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game has no accounts or login, no chat, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't use analytics or tracking services. The Game works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example coins, unlocked weapons, story stars, high scores, sound and control preferences) are saved only on your own device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes it.

Third parties

The Game is built with the Unity engine. We have turned off Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, crash reporting and hardware statistics, so the Game sends no data to Unity or any other third party. When you download the Game, Google Play may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The Game doesn't request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
