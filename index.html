Privacy Policy

Stickman Showdown, by Quillstorm Studios · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Stickman Showdown ("the Game"), published by Quillstorm Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: we don't collect your personal data ourselves, but the Game shows ads, and our ad partners collect limited device data to do that (see "Advertising" below).

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email or contacts. The Game has no accounts or login, no chat and no in-app purchases. Gameplay works offline; ads need an internet connection.

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource) and its partner ad networks, which may include Unity Ads and Google AdMob. Some ads are optional: you choose to watch them for a reward (for example double coins or a revive). When ads load or are shown, these providers may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise ads.

The first time you open the Game we ask whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Ad partner privacy policies: Unity · Google.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example coins, unlocked weapons, story stars, high scores, sound and control preferences) are saved only on your own device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes it.

Third parties

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics, crash reporting and hardware statistics are turned off. Apart from the ad partners described above, the Game sends no data to third parties. When you download the Game, Google Play may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The Game doesn't request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. It uses internet access and the advertising ID permission for ads.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
